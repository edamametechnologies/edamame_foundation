# Profiling Feedback Privacy Policy (EN)

By reporting an incorrect profiling for a given device, you agree to share the following information with EDAMAME, as seen in the device info pop-up:

- The device vendor name
- The device open ports and service banners/headers if available
- The device advertized mDNS services
- The device hostname

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not report an incorrect profiling.
