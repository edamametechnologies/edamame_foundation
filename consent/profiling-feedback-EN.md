# Profiling Feedback Privacy Policy (EN)

By reporting an incorrect profiling for a given device, you agree to share the following information with EDAMAME, as seen in the device info pop-up:

- The device type EDAMAME assigned to the device
- The device vendor name
- The device open ports, with the services, banners/headers and known vulnerabilities EDAMAME found for them
- The device advertized mDNS services
- The device hostname
- Your note, if you write one

EDAMAME stores the report with its date and the IP address it came from, and its team is notified of it in its Slack workspace.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not report an incorrect profiling.
