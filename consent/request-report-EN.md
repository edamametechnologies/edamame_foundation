# Certification Report Privacy Policy (EN)

By requesting a certification report, you send EDAMAME the email address you enter and the signature of a score report. EDAMAME Hub finds the score report that signature belongs to and:

- Emails the certification report to the address you entered, from certification@edamame.tech. The report shows the security score, the device identifier, the operating system, the username, the IP addresses, the approximate location (city, country and coordinates), the report and expiry dates, the compliance with security frameworks, the checks that pass and fail, and the signatures of the report and of its threat model. A report's signature is enough to request it: share it only with people who may see the report.
- Keeps the email address. Unless it is an edamame.tech address, EDAMAME also gives it to its email outreach provider, Apollo.io, which can send you follow-up emails.
- Notifies EDAMAME's team in its Slack workspace, with the device identifier, the username, the operating system, the score, the city and country, the report date and the email address.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not request a report.
