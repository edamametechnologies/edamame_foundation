# User Feedback Privacy Policy (EN)

By providing feedback, you agree to share the following information with EDAMAME:

- Your message, and your email address if you provide one
- The screen of the app you sent the feedback from
- The EDAMAME version, build and memory use, the name, date and signature of the threat model in use, and the state of the EDAMAME Helper
- Your operating system name and version, and your numerical Security Score
- The last 100 lines of the EDAMAME app log and, when the EDAMAME Helper runs, of its log. EDAMAME masks secrets and some identifiers in these lines, but they can still contain file paths, network addresses, host names and email addresses

EDAMAME's support team receives your feedback in its Slack workspace and by email, with the log lines attached. If you provide an email address, EDAMAME keeps it and its team can use it to answer you. It is not added to EDAMAME's email outreach.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not provide feedback.
