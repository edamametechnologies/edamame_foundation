# Important Notice: Legal Compliance when Using EDAMAME Security Scanner Feature

Before using the EDAMAME Security scanner feature, it is crucial to ensure that you are in full compliance with all applicable laws and regulations of the jurisdiction in which you are operating.

## Legal Compliance Advisory

The use of security scanning tools like the EDAMAME Security scanner can have legal implications, particularly when used on networks that you do not own or have explicit permission to scan.
Ensure that your use of the EDAMAME Security scanner feature does not violate any local, state, or federal laws, or any applicable cybersecurity regulations or guidelines.

## Recommended Precautions

Always seek explicit consent before scanning networks that are not under your direct ownership or control.
Familiarize yourself with the legal guidelines governing the use of security scanning tools in your specific geographical location and industry sector.
Consider consulting with a legal professional specialized in cybersecurity to ensure that your use of the EDAMAME Security scanner feature is legally compliant.

## Sharing with Other EDAMAME Devices
Once you agree, EDAMAME shares the devices it finds on this network with the other EDAMAME apps on the same network, and lists the devices they share. For each device, it sends its IP and MAC addresses, host name, vendor, type and operating system, the services it announces, its open ports and their service banners, and any name you gave it.
Whether or not you agree, the other EDAMAME apps on the network receive this device's host name, EDAMAME device identifier, operating system and version, EDAMAME version, security score, whether the EDAMAME Helper is installed, and its local IP addresses.

## Conclusion
While the EDAMAME Security scanner feature is a powerful tool for enhancing network security, it must be used responsibly and in accordance with all relevant legal standards. Failure to ensure legal compliance could result in severe legal penalties and repercussions.
